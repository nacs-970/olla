<objective>
Refactor `run_loop()` to accept optional externally-owned session state (D-01/D-02/D-05) and stand up the
thinnest real `prompt_toolkit`-backed REPL that drives it turn after turn, wired end-to-end from `olla`
with no TASK argument through to a persisted `Scratchpad`. This is the phase's tracer: every layer the
phase touches (CLI entry, REPL controller, `run_loop()`'s state ownership, `Scratchpad`'s lifetime
contract, the new dependencies) gets wired for real here, production-quality, before REPL-01's full UX
polish (07-02) and REPL-03's context trimming (07-03) expand on top of it.

Purpose: Prove the D-01 architecture (costly to reverse — it changes `run_loop()`'s public contract and
ownership model) works end-to-end on two consecutive real turns before any further REPL surface is built
on top of it.
Output: `prompt_toolkit`/`tiktoken` added as vetted dependencies; `SessionState` dataclass and
session-injectable `run_loop()`; minimal `src/olla/repl.py` entry point wired from `cli.py`; `Scratchpad`
docstring contract updated for its dual one-shot/session lifetime.
